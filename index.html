Field notes / Before you begin

A little setup.
Then you’re off.

01 / EXTRACT

Unzip. Then open.

Every project downloads as a ZIP. Extract the entire folder, keep its files together, and follow START-HERE.txt or the README to open the app, setup, or browser game.

02 / REQUIREMENTS

A little setup.

Desktop apps are for Windows x64. Some projects need a supported GPU, controllers, a separate driver, Minecraft with Fabric, or your own emulator firmware and games. Check the included guide.

03 / PREVIEWS

Know what’s ready.

Experiments and previews have limits. Calculator payments are inactive, Roblox integration is unfinished, and LaptopLink currently demonstrates synthetic video. Each download explains its status.

✳

These are personal projects and experiments. Expect a little setup, a little discovery, and the occasional rough edge.
